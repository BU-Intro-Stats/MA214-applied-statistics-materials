\subsection*{Purpose}
Today you will fit candidate Project 1 models, compare them, diagnose model issues, and decide what evidence supports your final modeling strategy.

\subsection*{Shared Group Workflow}
Work in one shared Google Doc and one shared analysis folder. Assign the four roles below, then have every member inspect the models and sign off on the interpretation.
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.25em]
  \item \textbf{Member 1 -- Modeling lead:} fit and document the candidate models.
  \item \textbf{Member 2 -- Comparison lead:} compare fit, prediction, and interpretability evidence.
  \item \textbf{Member 3 -- Diagnostics lead:} check assumptions, influential observations, and remedial options.
  \item \textbf{Member 4 -- Interpretation editor:} connect results to the research question and maintain the shared report.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{In-Class Work}
\begin{enumerate}[label={\bfseries (\Alph*)},itemsep=.5em]
  \item \textbf{Candidate models.} Fit linear, multiple regression, or logistic regression models as appropriate for your response variable.
  \item \textbf{Model comparison.} Compare candidate models using summaries such as adjusted $R^2$, AIC, residual behavior, prediction quality, or interpretability.
  \item \textbf{Diagnostics.} Check residual plots, influential observations, transformations, or classification summaries as appropriate.
  \item \textbf{Interpretation.} Translate key coefficients or predictions into language connected to your research question.
  \item \textbf{Check-in.} Which model is currently strongest, and what concern still needs to be addressed?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Deliverable}
Submit the Project 1 progress report through \submissionplatform{} by \duedate{}. Include candidate models, diagnostics, a justified modeling direction, and the accessible shared Google Doc link.
