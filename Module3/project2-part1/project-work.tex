\subsection*{Purpose}
Today your group will launch Project 2 and work directly on the \textbf{Deliverable 1 outline}. By the end of lab, your shared Google Doc should contain a complete first draft covering both the simulation study and real-data analysis.

\subsection*{Shared Google Doc Setup}
\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=.25em]
  \item Create one Google Doc for the group and name it \texttt{P2_GroupNumber_Outline}.
  \item Share the document with all four group members and the instructor/TA with editing access.
  \item Add all four names and BUIDs at the top. Use headings that match the Deliverable 1 outline.
  \item Work in the same document throughout lab; do not create four separate drafts.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Group-of-Four Roles}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.25em]
  \item \textbf{Member 1 -- Project lead:} background, motivation, and final integration.
  \item \textbf{Member 2 -- Simulation lead:} selected study 1, violation, data-generating process, and simulation plan.
  \item \textbf{Member 3 -- Simulation lead:} selected study 2, violation, data-generating process, and simulation plan.
  \item \textbf{Member 4 -- Real-data and editor lead:} dataset, variables, limitations, timeline, and final quality check.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{In-Class Work}
\begin{enumerate}[label={\bfseries (\Alph*)},itemsep=.5em]
  \item \textbf{Project orientation.} Review the Project 2 overview and rubric together; identify the required simulation and real-data components.
  \item \textbf{Bootstrap practice.} Implement or review a bootstrap confidence interval for a regression quantity and record what the code returns.
  \item \textbf{Study selection.} Choose two studies (A--D), explain the assumption violations, and justify why they are useful to compare.
  \item \textbf{Real-data plan.} Select a dataset, response $Y$, primary predictor $X$, source, and expected assumption violations.
  \item \textbf{Integrate the outline.} Add sample sizes, repetitions, data-generating processes, planned outputs, timeline, and roles to the shared document.
  \item \textbf{Check-in.} Ask the instructor/TA to review the two selected studies and the connection between the simulations and real-data analysis.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Deliverable}
Submit the completed Project 2 outline through \submissionplatform{} by \duedate{}. Your shared Google Doc must contain:
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.25em]
  \item project title, motivation, and all four group members;
  \item two selected simulation studies and a plan for each;
  \item the two core research questions and any group-specific hypotheses;
  \item the real-data source, variables, limitations, and expected violations; and
  \item timeline, responsibilities, and planned simulation/analysis outputs.
\end{itemize}
Before submitting, confirm that every member has contributed, the document is readable without comments, and the final link is accessible to the instructor/TA.
