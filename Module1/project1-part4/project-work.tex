\subsection*{Purpose}
Today you will refine your Project 1 analysis, prepare presentation materials, and practice communicating statistical results and limitations clearly.

\subsection*{Shared Group Workflow}
Use one shared Google Doc for the presentation script and one shared folder for slides, code, data, and figures. Assign the four roles below; every member must rehearse and review the final materials.
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=.25em]
  \item \textbf{Member 1 -- Story lead:} motivation, data, and opening slides.
  \item \textbf{Member 2 -- Results lead:} model results, key plots, and statistical interpretation.
  \item \textbf{Member 3 -- Presentation lead:} limitations, conclusion, timing, and rehearsal.
  \item \textbf{Member 4 -- Reproducibility editor:} code, figures, file organization, and final submission check.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{In-Class Work}
\begin{enumerate}[label={\bfseries (\Alph*)},itemsep=.5em]
  \item \textbf{Finalize results.} Confirm your final model, plots, and interpretation.
  \item \textbf{Build slides.} Prepare a concise presentation with motivation, data, method, results, limitations, and conclusion.
  \item \textbf{Practice.} Rehearse the presentation and identify where wording or visuals need revision.
  \item \textbf{Code and files.} Confirm that submitted code runs and that all figures or tables are reproducible.
  \item \textbf{Check-in.} What is the one result your audience should remember?
\end{enumerate}

\subsection*{Deliverable}
Submit the Project 1 final presentation materials or video through \submissionplatform{} by \duedate{}. Include the shared Google Doc/script link and confirm that all four members contributed and rehearsed.
